    <section id="aftercare">
          H2: Looking after your cabinets
          P: Most damage we get called out for comes from water, steam and the wrong cleaner, not from use. Find your finish below.
        <div class="care">
            H3: Melamine and wrapped doors
              LI: Wipe with a soft cloth and warm water with a drop of mild dish soap, then dry.
              LI: Wipe spills straight away, especially along joins and edging tape.
              LI: Keep kettles, air fryers and steam away from the underside of wall units.
            P: Avoid scourers, abrasive powders, bleach, solvents and leaving water to stand.
            H3: Spray-painted and lacquered
              LI: Wipe with a damp microfibre cloth and dry immediately.
              LI: Leave the paint [X weeks] to cure fully before anything heavier than a damp cloth.
              LI: Test any new cleaner on the inside of a door first.
            P: Avoid magic erasers, abrasive pads, ammonia and alcohol-based sprays. They dull the sheen.
            H3: Solid wood and veneer
              LI: Dust regularly and wipe with a barely damp cloth along the grain.
              LI: Use coasters and trivets. Keep pieces out of direct sun where you can.
              LI: Re-oil oiled finishes every [X months] with the oil we leave you.
            P: Don't oil a sealed or painted surface, soak veneer edges or use silicone furniture polish.
            H3: Hinges, runners and daily use
              LI: Close doors and drawers gently and keep loads within what the runners are rated for.
              LI: Keep sink and vanity bases dry. Report a plumbing leak as soon as you see it.
              LI: A dropped door is usually a hinge adjustment. Call us if you're not sure.
            P: Don't stand on drawers or hang weight from open doors.
          P: It's on your handover sheet, or send us a photo on WhatsApp. Until you know, stick to a dry, soft cloth and skip the cleaners and oils.
